- Familiarity with enterprise security, OAuth, key vault management, and HIPAA compliance considerations

NICE TO HAVE:
- Experience with React UI integrations or frontend chat widgets
- Background in healthcare IT or clinical data workflows (EHRs, FHIR)
- Experience with Docker and cloud serverless deployments (GCP Cloud Run or Azure)
